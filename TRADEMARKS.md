# Fennek OS name and logo

Fennek OS's **code** is free software under the [GPL-3.0](LICENSE): you may use, study, change and share it.

The **name "Fennek OS"** and the **fennec fox logo** are not covered by that license. They belong to the
Fennek OS project (maintainer: [@Olexify](https://github.com/Olexify)). They tell people they're getting the
real, official Fennek OS, built and signed by this project.

## Things you can do without asking

- Say that your work is **based on Fennek OS**, uses it, or is compatible with it, as long as it's clear
  you aren't the Fennek OS project.
- Write about, review, teach or make videos about Fennek OS, using the name and logo to refer to it.
- Run unmodified official builds anywhere, including in companies, schools and VMs.
- Contribute changes back to this repository.

## Things that need permission

- **Distributing a modified version under the name "Fennek OS"** or with the fox logo. If you ship your own
  build or fork, give it **a different name and logo**, and remove ours from:
  - `/usr/lib/os-release` (`NAME`, `PRETTY_NAME`, `LOGO`)
  - `system_files/usr/share/icons/hicolor/scalable/apps/fennek.svg`
  - the wallpaper, boot splash and Start-menu icon
- Using the name or logo for another product, a company, a domain, or merchandise.
- Implying that the Fennek OS project endorses your product.

## Why

Anyone can fork the code; that's the point of open source. The name has to mean one thing: the official,
signed Fennek OS that people can trust to have no telemetry and to get security fixes. Fedora, Firefox and
Ubuntu follow the same rule.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue at https://github.com/Olexify/FennekOS/issues.
